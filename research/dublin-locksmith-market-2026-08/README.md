## Method & limitations

Advertiser data comes from Google's Ads Transparency Center (region: Ireland, last 30 days) — Google's
own disclosure data, authoritative for who advertises and with what creatives, but it does not expose
per-keyword ad positions. Live Google.ie SERP captures were blocked by Google's automated-traffic checks
from the research network; verifying exact ad slots per keyword requires an Irish IP or a rank tracker
with an Irish location. Local rankings were captured live from Google Maps. Organic orderings are
indicative, compiled from Irish-intent web-index results. CPC figures in the report are labelled
industry estimates.
